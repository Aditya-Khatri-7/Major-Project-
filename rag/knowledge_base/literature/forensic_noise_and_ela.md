# Noise-residual and error-level analysis (ELA)

Noise-residual analysis removes image content with a smoothing filter and studies what is left: sensor noise and processing traces. In an untouched camera photo the noise level is fairly consistent across similar regions. A spliced or synthesised region often has a different noise level.

Error-level analysis re-saves the image as JPEG and compares it with the original. Regions with a different compression history respond differently.

## Reliability
Both are weak, supporting methods. Natural scene changes (sky vs foliage, shadows, sharpened edges) also change noise and ELA response, and social-media recompression removes much of the signal. A strong inconsistency is a reason to look closer, never a conclusion on its own.
