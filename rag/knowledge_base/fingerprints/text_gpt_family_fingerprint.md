# Generator fingerprint: GPT-style chat models (text)

Typical traits of instruction-tuned chat models such as the GPT family: fluent, well-organised and
even prose; balanced "on the one hand / on the other hand" framing; frequent hedges and signposting
phrases ("it is important to note", "furthermore", "overall", "in conclusion"); a tidy
intro-body-summary shape; bullet or numbered structure when the question allows it; neutral tone with
little personal anecdote or idiosyncratic detail; and low sentence-length variation (low burstiness).

These are tendencies, not rules. They vary with model version, prompt and system instructions, and a
human writing formally can show many of them. Use them as supporting signals only.
