"Look not unto the idols of the nations,
The work of fingers, upon the clay,
Molded by the hands of man's desire,
Whose breath departs and he forgets its ways.

Such is the fleeting glory of the world,
The empty vanity that passes by,
But unto us, the Word of God is given,
A treasure more precious than silver or gold.

For in His Word, we find the answer to all,
The light that guides us through the darkest night,
A firm foundation on which we can stand,
Unshakable, amidst the shifting sands.

It is our compass, pointing us to the way,
Guiding us through the maze of life's mysteries,
A lamp, shining bright in the darkest place,
Revealing truth, where lies and deceit prosper.

Do not be deceived by the transient beauty of the world,
For its allure will fade and its glory will depart,
But the Word of God, it endures forever,
A wellspring of life, that will never depart.

So let us hold fast to the Word,
And let its wisdom guide us day by day,
For in its truth, we find our hope and our salvation,
A light that shines, beyond the reach of any cultural decay."
